INSIDE THE STARTER

Four pages.
A clear purpose.

The exact page structure is agreed with you. Here’s a common starting point for a local service business.

Home

Introduce your business, explain the main offer, and give visitors a clear next step.

Services

Explain what you offer, where you work, and what customers should know before getting in touch.

About or project gallery

Tell your story or showcase your work using photos and information you provide.

Contact

Bring your contact details together with a direct email link or an agreed contact form.

Scope and payment

Online stores, booking systems, customer accounts, custom integrations, photography, and extensive copywriting need a separate quote. Domain, paid hosting, and third-party subscriptions are separate. Search setup does not include a ranking guarantee.

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we begin.

What do you need from me?＋

Your business details, services, logo if you have one, and any photos or copy you’d like to use. We’ll work out what’s missing before the project starts.

How long will my website take?＋

We’ll agree on a timeline after reviewing the scope and your content. The schedule includes time for you to review the site and request revisions.

Will I own my website?＋

Yes. Once the project is paid in full, you receive your website files. Your domain and hosting should be in accounts you control. Any third-party assets remain subject to their own licenses.

Can you improve an existing website?＋

Absolutely. Send the link and tell me what isn’t working for you. I’ll review the site and suggest a scope before quoting the work.
