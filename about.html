HOW WE WORK TOGETHER

A clear path
from hello to launch.

Here’s what you can expect at each stage, and where your input matters.

  1. Understand the business

    We talk through your services, customers, current website, and the action you want visitors to take. Bring your goals and any examples you like.

    You receive: an agreed page list, scope, price, and schedule.

  2. Shape the content and design

    You share your logo, photos, business details, and available copy. I organize that material and build a visual direction around it.

    You review: the structure, look, and wording.

  3. Build and refine

    I develop the pages and share a working preview. You collect your feedback, and we work through the revision rounds included in your package.

    You review: the actual site on your phone and computer.

  4. Launch and hand over

    We check page layouts, links, and the agreed inquiry route. Once the site is approved and the final payment is complete, we launch it and hand over the files.

    You receive: the finished website and control of your accounts.

IS THIS A GOOD FIT?
