Before we get started

A few practical questions.

What happens in the first conversation?

Discuss your business, current software, account volume, and the months you need help with. Those details shape a written scope before work begins.

Is this tax filing or financial advice?

This concept describes bookkeeping services only. Tax filing, legal advice, and investment advice are not included.

What would a handoff look like?

Agree on the records, access, reporting schedule, and responsibilities in advance. Secure access should be arranged separately, never through a public inquiry form.
