A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we begin.

What do you need from me?＋

Your business details, services, logo if you have one, and any photos or copy you’d like to use. We’ll work out what’s missing before the project starts.

How long will my website take?＋

We’ll agree on a timeline after reviewing the scope and your content. The schedule includes time for you to review the site and request revisions.

Will I own my website?＋

Yes. Once the project is paid in full, you receive your website files. Your domain and hosting should be in accounts you control. Any third-party assets remain subject to their own licenses.

Can you improve an existing website?＋

Absolutely. Send the link and tell me what isn’t working for you. I’ll review the site and suggest a scope before quoting the work.
